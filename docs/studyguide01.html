Process Arrival Time    Run Time

C       10 ms           40 ms

Note: For all policies, if multiple processes arrive at the same time, then schedule in alphabetical order (ie. A before B).

Note: For Round Robin add new processes to the waiting queue first and then rotate.

Note: For MLFQ, Q0 has a timeslice of 10ms, Q1 has a timeslice of 20ms, and Q2 has a timeslice of 40ms.
